Logistics: How We Service Springfield

From our Iowa shop, Springfield is a 462-mile haul (about 8 hours by truck) south. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel to Springfield, Missouri for field service?

From our Iowa shop, Springfield is a 462-mile haul. The economics typically favor ship-in service; we do schedule field visits for substantial jobs after scoping the travel.

Does Midwest CNC support Springfield's healthcare sector?

Yes. Springfield machinists working in healthcare and adjacent sectors are a routine part of our customer base. The Mazak and Haas platforms common in that work fall squarely in our brand coverage.

What's the shipping process for Springfield spindles coming in for rebuild?

Standard freight handles it. Springfield-to-Waterloo transit is typically 3–5 business days. We include return shipping in the rebuild quote.

What lead time should a Springfield customer expect?

Most CNC machine repair and spindle rebuilds return to Springfield within 3–6 weeks depending on brand, failure mode, and parts availability. Way-cover builds typically ship in 2–4 weeks.
