Common Questions

How fast can you respond to a quote request?

Most quote requests go out within one business day. If the machine is down and you need someone on the line right now, call 319-610-4341 — we'd rather talk through the symptoms than wait for an email back. Field troubleshooting on the phone often saves a full teardown.

Do you work on older or discontinued machines?

Yes. We have dedicated brand pages and parts-sourcing approaches for Fadal, Hitachi Seiki, and Monarch — manufacturers whose CNC lines have been gone for years but whose machines are still cutting daily in Midwest shops. We lean on aftermarket suppliers, used-market sourcing, and custom-machined replacements where OEM components are gone.

Where do you work? Do you come on-site?

We serve shops across Iowa, Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Nebraska, Missouri, and Texas from our Waterloo, Iowa shop. Field service when that's the right call for the job; spindle rebuilds and way-cover builds happen at our bench and ship back to you.

Can you recover a crashed spindle?

Often, yes. Crash recovery is one of the threads of work that comes through here — taper damage, bearing-pack failure, alignment loss from impact. Customers are usually relieved to avoid the replacement lead times and the six-figure capital expense of a new spindle. Bring it in for a teardown.
