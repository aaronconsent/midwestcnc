## Common failure patterns

- ATC wear from production cycles — solenoid and pawl alignment.
- Spindle thermal issues — heat-related drift on heavy cuts.
- Control panel reliability — keypad and touchscreen on older Genos.
- Way wear in dirty production environments.

## Controls used on this series

Genos ships on [OSP-P300](/repairs/okuma-cnc-machine-repair/osp-p300/) — the standard control for the line through current builds.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
