## What Brings Makino Machines In For Repair

Most Makino repair calls come in for way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, ATC faults, or drive system wear. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Makino Repair Work

Makino control systems vary by model, so we start with diagnostics that match the platform in front of us rather than assuming what we'll find.
