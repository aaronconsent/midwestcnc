## Common failure patterns

- Trunnion calibration drift after a crash — A and C axis zero-point recovery.
- Swivel head bearing wear on monoBLOCK builds.
- RTCP and kinematic drift after any 5-axis component work.
- Swarf intrusion at trunnion bearings on heavy-coolant production.

## Controls used on this series

DMU machines mostly ship on [Heidenhain TNC](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/heidenhain-tnc/) (iTNC 530 on legacy builds, TNC 640 on current). DMC builds typically ship on [Siemens 840D](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/siemens-840d/). All run under [CELOS](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/celos/).

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
