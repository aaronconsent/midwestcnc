## Common failure patterns

- ATC carousel faults — solenoid, indexer pawl, carousel-position drift.
- Ballscrew wear from sustained production.
- Spindle bearing failure on high-RPM production work.
- Way cover damage from chip intrusion or crash.

## Controls used on this series

DNM ships on [Fanuc 0i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-0i/) on entry and mid-range builds, and [Fanuc 30i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-30i-31i-32i/) on the higher-end DNM 4000/5700/6700/750 production verticals.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
