## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>Puma V400</li><li>Puma V550</li><li>Puma V700</li><li>Puma VT 1100</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- Large-table bearing wear from sustained heavy-cut production.
- ATC reliability on the V-series with integrated milling.
- Hydraulic clamp pressure loss on the workpiece clamping system.
- Way wear on the long ram travel of VTR machines.

## Controls used on this series

Puma V, VT, and VTR ship on [Fanuc 30i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-30i-31i-32i/) on most current builds. Older V400 may still run [Fanuc 0i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-0i/).

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
