## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>LAW 1000</li><li>LAW 2000</li><li>LAW 3000</li><li>LFS-590</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- Large-bore spindle wear on sustained heavy roughing.
- Way wear from extended heavy-cut production.
- Hydraulic chuck pressure loss on large workpieces.
- Drive amplifier faults from heavy-cut loads.

## Controls used on this series

LAW and LFS run on [OSP-P200](/repairs/okuma-cnc-machine-repair/osp-p200/) on older builds and [OSP-P300](/repairs/okuma-cnc-machine-repair/osp-p300/) on current.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
