## What Brings Okuma Machines In For Repair

Most Okuma repair calls come in for control problems, ATC faults, drive system wear, way alignment, or spindle issues. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Okuma Repair Work

Okuma machines run OSP, so diagnostics need to come from someone who knows the platform — that's us.
