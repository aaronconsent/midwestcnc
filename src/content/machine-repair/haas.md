## What Brings Haas Machines In For Repair

Most Haas repair calls come in for drive system wear, way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, or ATC faults. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Haas Repair Work

Haas machines run Haas control, so diagnostics need to come from someone who knows the platform — that's us.
