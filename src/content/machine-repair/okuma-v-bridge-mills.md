## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>MU-400V</li><li>MU-500V</li><li>MU-6300V</li><li>MU-8000V</li><li>MCR-A5C</li><li>MCR-BIII</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- Trunnion calibration drift on MU builds — A and C-axis zero-point work.
- Bridge geometry calibration on MCR — large-span alignment.
- Spindle bearing wear on MU 5-axis builds from high-stress cuts.
- Linear scale issues on bridge machines.

## Controls used on this series

MU and MCR ship on [OSP-P300](/repairs/okuma-cnc-machine-repair/osp-p300/) — the standard control for these platforms across the current generation.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
