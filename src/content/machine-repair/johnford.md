## What Brings Johnford Machines In For Repair

Most Johnford repair calls come in for drive system wear, way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, or ATC faults. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.
