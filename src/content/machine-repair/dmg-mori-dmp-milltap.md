## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>DMP 35</li><li>DMP 50</li><li>DMP 70</li><li>Milltap 700</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- High-cycle ATC wear from short-cycle production.
- Ballscrew wear on heavy-throughput drill-tap work.
- Spindle bearing failure on high-RPM, short-cycle use.
- Way wear in dirty production environments.

## Controls used on this series

DMP and Milltap ship on [Siemens 840D](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/siemens-840d/) under [CELOS](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/celos/).
