## Common failure patterns

- B-axis milling spindle bearing wear — the highest-stress component on these platforms.
- Sub-spindle synchronization drift on multi-tasking part transfer.
- Tool changer reliability — heavy ATC use on long-cycle parts.
- Spindle drive faults from sustained heavy cuts.

## Controls used on this series

NTX ships on [Siemens 840D](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/siemens-840d/) under [CELOS](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/celos/). The B-axis kinematics need re-calibration after any milling-spindle work.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
