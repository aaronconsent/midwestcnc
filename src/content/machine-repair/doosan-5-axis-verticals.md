## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>DVF 5000</li><li>DVF 6500</li><li>DVF 8000</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- Trunnion calibration drift after a crash.
- A and C-axis encoder faults — contamination or signal loss.
- RTCP drift after spindle or trunnion work.
- Linear-motor drive issues on the FM 200/5AX.

## Controls used on this series

DVF and FM ship on [Fanuc 30i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-30i-31i-32i/) — typically 30i-B on current builds. The 5-axis kinematics need 30i's higher feature set.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
