## What Brings Brother Machines In For Repair

Most Brother repair calls come in for drive system wear, way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, or ATC faults. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Brother Repair Work

Brother control systems vary by model, so we start with diagnostics that match the platform in front of us rather than assuming what we'll find.
