## What Brings Giddings & Lewis Machines In For Repair

Most Giddings & Lewis repair calls come in for way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, ATC faults, or drive system wear. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Giddings & Lewis Repair Work

Giddings & Lewis control systems vary by model, so we start with diagnostics that match the platform in front of us rather than assuming what we'll find.
