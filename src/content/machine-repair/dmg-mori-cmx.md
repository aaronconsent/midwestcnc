## Common failure patterns

- ATC reliability — solenoid wear and pawl alignment on heavy-cycle work.
- Spindle bearing wear on machines pushed to upper RPM limits.
- Way cover damage in busy production environments.
- Coolant intrusion at the spindle nose on high-coolant work.

## Controls used on this series

CMX machines ship on [Siemens 840D](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/siemens-840d/) under [CELOS](/repairs/dmg-mori-cnc-machine-repair/celos/).

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
