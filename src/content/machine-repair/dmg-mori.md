## What Brings DMG Mori Machines In For Repair

Most DMG Mori repair calls come in for control problems, ATC faults, drive system wear, way alignment, or spindle issues. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach DMG Mori Repair Work

DMG Mori machines run Siemens / Heidenhain (varies by line), so diagnostics need to come from someone who knows the platform — that's us.
