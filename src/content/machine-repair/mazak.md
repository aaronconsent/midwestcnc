## What Brings Mazak Machines In For Repair

Most Mazak repair calls come in for ATC faults, drive system wear, way alignment, spindle issues, or control problems. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Mazak Repair Work

Mazak machines run MAZATROL, so diagnostics need to come from someone who knows the platform — that's us.
