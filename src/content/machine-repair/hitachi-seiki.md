## What Brings Hitachi Seiki Machines In For Repair

Most Hitachi Seiki repair calls come in for drive system wear, way alignment, spindle issues, control problems, or ATC faults. We diagnose what's actually broken before we quote — sometimes what looks like a spindle problem is something cheaper to fix.

## How We Approach Hitachi Seiki Repair Work

Hitachi Seiki control systems vary by model, so we start with diagnostics that match the platform in front of us rather than assuming what we'll find.
