## Common failure patterns

- Turret indexing — the most common single failure on Lynx compact platforms.
- Bar feeder integration issues — sync and bar-end detection.
- Sub-spindle alignment on LSY configurations.
- Chuck cylinder leaks from sustained bar-work cycles.

## Controls used on this series

Lynx ships on [Fanuc 0i](/repairs/fanuc-cnc-machine-repair/series-0i/) — typically 0i-D or 0i-F. The compact platform doesn't need the higher-end 30i family.

## Lead time

Lead time depends on the model, the failure mode, and parts availability. Diagnostic is fast; full rebuilds run 3 to 5 weeks on most jobs. We scope each job individually rather than quoting a generic window.
