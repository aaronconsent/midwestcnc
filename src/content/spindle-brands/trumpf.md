# Trumpf Laser & Punch Service

Trumpf systems tend to come in with optics contamination, resonator issues on older CO2 machines, and cooling-related laser faults. We service laser source and optics work on TruLaser and older CO2 systems, plus drive and tooling work on TruPunch machines — most jobs run 2–6 weeks.
