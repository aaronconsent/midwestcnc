## Models in this series we service

<ul class="model-chips"><li>HCN-4000</li><li>HCN-5000</li><li>HCN-6000</li><li>HCN-8800</li><li>HCN-10800</li><li>PFH</li><li>H-Series</li></ul>

## Common failure patterns

- Bearing-pack wear from sustained pallet-changer production cycles on HCN-4000 through HCN-6000.
- Larger spindle bearing wear on HCN-8800 and HCN-10800 from heavy axial cuts.
- Taper damage from toolholder issues during the high-cycle ATC sequence.
- Spindle thermal drift on machines running long-cycle finishing work without spindle warm-up.
- Encoder issues from thermal cycling at the pallet-changer interface.

## Controls used on this series

Mid-2000s HCN runs on [Matrix](/spindle-grinding/mazak-spindle-repair/mazatrol-matrix/); current HCN-8800 and HCN-10800 ship on [SmoothX](/spindle-grinding/mazak-spindle-repair/smooth-control/). Legacy PFH and H-series machines run [Mazatrol Legacy](/spindle-grinding/mazak-spindle-repair/mazatrol-legacy/) controls and the spindle parts situation reflects late-life status.
