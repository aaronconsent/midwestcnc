# Mori Seiki Spindle Repair & Rebuilds

What we see most on Mori Seiki spindles: older belt-drive setups tend to come in with pulley wear and tired bearings. Newer direct-drive units usually show heat damage or preload loss. We rebuild, regrind, and rebalance across the Mori Seiki platform — SL series, NL series, older MV mills, and NH horizontals — with most jobs running 3–5 weeks and field troubleshooting where it can save a teardown.
